# Proposal: pause and seek from the picture, and ring the focused scrubber thumb (T8-27)

- Status: filed for the owner, 2026-11-27. Not approved. It rests on the Product Owner's calls 4 and 5 of `docs/specs/decisions-product-owner-2026-11-26c.md`, listed in `docs/specs/awaiting-owner.md`. Under `AGENTS.md`'s rule for guiding documents, T8-27's approved task is followed while this proposal is open.
- The thumb's size (24 px) is already in `DESIGN.md`, approved on its own (`docs/proposals/scrub-thumb.md`). This proposal does not touch §8.

## What is wrong

T8-27 changes what the remote's keys do while only the picture shows, and how the focused scrubber looks. `DESIGN.md` still describes T8-04's behaviour:

- **7.14, Remote keys.** "An arrow wakes the chrome with focus on the scrubber and does not seek." The app now seeks 10 s on a Left or Right from the bare picture. "Select with the chrome hidden … else wakes the chrome on Play or Pause." The app now pauses as well.
- **7.14, item 2 (Scrubber).** The thumb has no focus ring. The app now draws the two-tone focus ring around it.
- **7.14, As built: Scrubber, Focus holder, Keys.** They say the thumb has "no ring", that the holder's keys "neither seek nor pause", and nothing about a held arrow from the picture.
- **Section 9, Remote keys table.** "Player | Arrows" says the first arrow only wakes the chrome; "Player, chrome hidden | Select" ends "else show the controls".

Section 12 needs no change: none of its player rules is affected, and the calls themselves are listed in `docs/specs/awaiting-owner.md`.

## The exact new text

Every passage below is in a TV-specific part of `DESIGN.md` (the player's remote keys and the remote-keys table). No text is proposed for a shared section.

### 7.14, item 2 (Scrubber)

Current:

> a 4 px track that grows to 8 px when focused, elapsed time left and remaining time right ("−1:21:40") in `typeMono`, chapters as 2 px gaps, the buffered range as a lighter bar ahead of the playhead, an `artAccent` fill, and a 24 px `text1` thumb when focused.

New:

> a 4 px track that grows to 8 px when focused, elapsed time left and remaining time right ("−1:21:40") in `typeMono`, chapters as 2 px gaps, the buffered range as a lighter bar ahead of the playhead, an `artAccent` fill, and a 24 px `text1` thumb with the focus ring when focused.

The sentence on the scrub preview that follows is unchanged.

### 7.14, Remote keys

Current:

> **Remote keys.** An arrow wakes the chrome with focus on the scrubber and does not seek. Left and Right then seek 10 s. Holding Left or Right for half a second pauses and scrubs a pending position in growing steps (10 s per 200 ms tick, then 20, 60, and 120 s, never more than a fiftieth of the duration) with the preview following; select commits and Back returns to where playback was. Up and Down move between the top bar, the scrubber, and the control row, then to a visible Up Next card. Each zone remembers its last control. Select with the chrome hidden activates a visible Skip, else Play now on a visible Up Next card, else wakes the chrome on Play or Pause. Play/Pause on the remote always toggles.

New:

> **Remote keys.** With the chrome hidden and no Skip or Up Next showing, Left and Right seek 10 s and wake the chrome with focus on the scrubber; Up and Down wake it there without seeking. With the scrubber focused, Left and Right seek 10 s. Holding Left or Right for half a second pauses and scrubs a pending position in growing steps (10 s per 200 ms tick, then 20, 60, and 120 s, never more than a fiftieth of the duration) with the preview following, whether the chrome was showing or not; select commits and Back returns to where playback was. Up and Down move between the top bar, the scrubber, and the control row, then to a visible Up Next card. Each zone remembers its last control. Select with the chrome hidden activates a visible Skip, else Play now on a visible Up Next card, else pauses and wakes the chrome on Play or Pause. Play/Pause on the remote always toggles.

Skip, then Play now, stay ahead of the pause for select.

### 7.14, As built: Scrubber

Current:

> - **Scrubber.** Focus shows as the track growing from `scrubTrackH` to `scrubTrackHActive` and the `scrubThumb` thumb in `text1`, with no ring and no lift; with no known duration there is no thumb and no remaining time. …

New:

> - **Scrubber.** Focus shows as the track growing from `scrubTrackH` to `scrubTrackHActive` and the `scrubThumb` thumb in `text1` carrying the two-tone focus ring, with no lift (the scrubber is a row, section 9); with no known duration there is no thumb and no remaining time. …

The rest of the bullet is unchanged.

### 7.14, As built: Focus holder

Current:

> - **Focus holder.** While only the picture shows, an invisible full-screen element, "Player controls" to TalkBack, holds focus. It is the one place no focus is visible (section 9). An arrow shows the chrome with focus on the scrubber; select shows it with focus on Play or Pause. Neither seeks nor pauses.

New:

> - **Focus holder.** While only the picture shows, an invisible full-screen element, "Player controls" to TalkBack, holds focus. It is the one place no focus is visible (section 9). Select pauses and shows the chrome with focus on Play or Pause; on a picture already paused it resumes and the chrome stays hidden. Left and Right seek 10 s and show the chrome with focus on the scrubber; Up and Down only show it there. Before the first frame the holder's keys do nothing.

### 7.14, As built: Keys

Current:

> - **Keys.** … A held arrow shows the chrome or keeps it up and does not seek: hold-to-scrub is not built. A key does nothing until it comes up. …

New:

> - **Keys.** … A held arrow shows the chrome or keeps it up and does not seek, from the picture too: hold-to-scrub is not built. A key does nothing until it comes up, so a pause or a step from the picture lands when the key is released. …

The rest of the bullet is unchanged.

### Section 9, Remote keys table

Current:

> | Player | Arrows | Wake the chrome on the scrubber; then Left and Right seek 10 s, Up and Down move between zones |
>
> | Player, chrome hidden | Select | Skip, else Play now, else show the controls |

New:

> | Player | Arrows | Chrome hidden: Left and Right seek 10 s and show the chrome on the scrubber, Up and Down show it there; then Left and Right on the scrubber seek 10 s, Up and Down move between zones |

> | Player, chrome hidden | Select | Skip, else Play now, else pause and show the controls |
