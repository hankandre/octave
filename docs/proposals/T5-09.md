# Proposal (T5-09): focus on Home's hero and at a row's end

Not approved. Only the owner can approve it. `DESIGN.md` is unchanged.

## What is wrong or missing

`DESIGN.md` does not say where focus goes at two edges of Home, and one of its rules reads as if it did.

- **Up into the hero.** §7.4 and §9 say Up and Down across rows go to the card nearest by horizontal centre. The hero is not a row of cards: it is two buttons at the left, with one obvious action (principle 2). Nearest centre sends Up from most cards to More info. Nothing says that Home then shows the whole hero.
- **Right past the hero's last button** is not covered.
- **A row's last card.** §9 keeps the focused card 96 px from any edge and says nothing covers a focus ring. The right edge's fade, as §12 records it, does not say what happens when the last card is focused, where the fade would cover its ring and caption.

## Exact new text

### §7.3 Hero

Replace:

> - Focus opens on Resume. The title the hero shows is not repeated as the first card of the row below it.

with:

> - Focus opens on Resume. The title the hero shows is not repeated as the first card of the row below it.
> - Up from any card of the first row lands on the primary action (More info when the hero has no primary), and Home scrolls to its top, so the title, facts and overview are in view. Right on the hero's last button does nothing: focus stays on it and nothing scrolls.

### §7.4 Home and its rows

In the bullet that starts "A row is a title", replace:

> Up and Down move to the adjacent row, to the card nearest by horizontal centre. The focused card keeps 96 px from the edge.

with:

> Up and Down move to the adjacent row, to the card nearest by horizontal centre; Up from the first row into the hero is the exception (7.3). The focused card keeps 96 px from the edge, the last card too: a row has room after its last card for that margin, and its end fade is off while that card has focus.

### §9 Interaction rules, "Focus model"

Replace:

> - Rows and grids move focus within themselves with Left and Right; Up and Down across rows go to the nearest item by horizontal centre. Left from the first item of a screen opens the rail; Right from the rail returns to where focus was.

with:

> - Rows and grids move focus within themselves with Left and Right; Up and Down across rows go to the nearest item by horizontal centre. Up into Home's hero is the exception: it lands on the hero's primary action (7.3). Left from the first item of a screen opens the rail; Right from the rail returns to where focus was.

Replace:

> - Focus is never hidden: rows scroll the focused card to at least 96 px from any edge, and the rail never covers it.

with:

> - Focus is never hidden: rows scroll the focused card to at least 96 px from any edge, and the rail never covers it. No fade covers a row's focused last card.

### Wording at the end of a row

The text states the margin a viewer sees (at least 96 px) and gives no length for the row's end padding. It stays true whether the app ends a row exactly 96 px after its last card or a device pixel further.

### §12

No new text. The guiding-document rules in `AGENTS.md` keep per-task detail and history out of `DESIGN.md`. The reasons are calls 3 and 4 of `docs/specs/decisions-product-owner-2026-11-26d.md`, which are provisional; if the owner approves this text, the pull request that applies it adds the record under `docs/decisions/`.
