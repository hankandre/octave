## Goal

Right on the hero's last button stays put; Up from Home's first row lands on the hero's primary action with the whole hero in view; a row's focused last card is clear of the fade and 96 px in. Down from the hero, Up and Down between rows, each row's remembered place and the rail behave as today.

## Causes (established from source)

Sources: react-native-tvos 0.88.1-0 as installed (`apps/tv/node_modules/react-native/ReactAndroid/src/main/java/com/facebook/react/views/…`), Android's `FocusFinder` and `HorizontalScrollView` (AOSP `platform_frameworks_base`, main), and the app code. Coordinates are reasoned from the layout code, not measured.

1. **Focus guides.** A `TVFocusGuideView` with destinations offers the search only itself while focus is outside it, and sends focus to its destination (`view/ReactViewGroup.kt:1311-1319` `isTVFocusGuide`, `:1362-1383` `addFocusables`, `:1442-1484` `requestFocus`). With no destinations its children are searched one by one. A `trapFocus*` direction runs the search inside the guide only, so nothing is found past its edge (`:1486-1506` `focusSearch`). Traps do not make a view a guide.
2. **F3, Right from More info goes to Recently added's second card.** Hero buttons have no guide and no trap (`home/hero.tsx:87-104`), so Right searches the whole window. `FocusFinder.isCandidate` for Right needs `src.left < dest.left`:
   - Continue watching's guide spans the row from the content's left edge, left of More info, so it is not a candidate.
   - Focusing a hero button calls `fromHeader`, which clears every guide and points only the first row's (`design/rows/row-list.tsx:61-68`, `row-router.ts` `clear`, `enter`). Recently added's guide has no destination, so its cards are searched one by one. Card 1 starts at `rowInsetStart + 248 + 24` = 296 canvas px into the content, right of More info's left edge, and is the nearest candidate. That is the review's "card 2".
3. **F4, Up from a first-row card lands on More info with the title off screen.**
   - Up from the first row is left to geometry: the router points only adjacent rows, and the hero is not a row (`row-router.ts` `focused`).
   - The only candidates above are the two hero buttons. T3-43's `rail-guide` is not one: Up needs `src.bottom > dest.bottom`, and the rail is full height.
   - From any card past the first, neither button is in the card's beam, and More info's centre is nearer, so it wins. From the first card both are in the beam, and the nearer centre wins.
   - Up is handled first by the vertical list itself: `ScrollView.executeKeyEvent` → `arrowScroll(FOCUS_UP)` (AOSP; `scroll/ReactScrollView.kt:513-538`, `:750-758`). It finds the next focus inside the list and smooth-scrolls (`doScrollY`) only enough to show that view's rect, then requests focus on it. `scrollAnimationEnabled` defaults to true (`:215`), so `computeScroll` animates rather than jumps (`:575-587`). A focus that does not come by an arrow scrolls through `requestChildFocus` → `scrollToChild` (`:546-552`). Nothing in Home scrolls to the top (`home/home-screen.tsx`, `hero.tsx`), so the title and overview above the buttons stay off screen.
   - A later JS `scrollToOffset(0, animated: false)` reaches `scrollTo` (`:1241-1245`), which does not stop a running `OverScroller`; the next `computeScroll` would carry on to the animation's target. So the scroll that Up starts must itself end at the top.
4. **F5, the fade covers the last card.**
   - (a) **The fade never turns off.** RN draws the end fade at a constant strength, `fadingEdgeLengthEnd / max(start, end)` = 1, whatever the scroll position (`scroll/ReactHorizontalScrollView.kt:425-437`). Android's own strength falls to 0 at the end. So a row with nothing more to its right is still faded.
   - (b) **The last card cannot reach 96 px.** Focus scrolling keeps a fading-edge length of room only while the card's right edge is short of the content's end (`HorizontalScrollView.computeScrollDeltaToGetChildRectOnScreen`, used through `ReactHorizontalScrollView.kt:350-355`, `:603-611`, `:682-687`). The scroll is then clamped at the maximum. The content ends `cardGap` (24) after the last card's box: cells have `paddingRight: cardGap`, and the list has no end padding (`media-row.tsx:143`, `:169`). So the last card stops 24 px from the row's end. With the lift and ring (about 24 px more) it sits at or past the edge, under the full fade. That matches the review's 12 to 42 px.
   - (c) **The fade length sets the margin.** The manager turns a fade length of 0 into no fading edge at all (`ReactHorizontalScrollViewManager.kt:403-411`), and with it goes the 96 px focus margin on both sides. That is why §12's fade "keeps the focused card 96 px in" on the left too.
   - The row's viewport ends at the screen's right edge: `shell-content` is `flex: 1` after the rail (`shell/app-shell.tsx:205`, `:229`).

## Approach

- **One guide around the whole hero, not `nextFocusUp` on cards, and not around the action bar alone.** A bar-only guide would let `arrowScroll` smooth-scroll to the bar, and that animation would undo `revealHeader` (cause 3). One rect catches Up from every card, needs no tag plumbing into the rows, and T3-08's frozen tests forbid `nextFocus*` in rows (`media-row.test.tsx:76`, `row-list.test.tsx:113`). The guide uses `destinations`, not `autoFocus`: `autoFocus` returns to the button focused last.
- **Point it with `pointGuide`.** `shell/focus-bridge` is already Home's dependency direction (Home imports `shell/routes`, `shell/shell-context`), and the frozen T3-09 double records the calls. T3-43's test counts only calls on `rail-guide`.
- **Scroll to the top in JS, instantly, for focus that does not come by Up.** On Up the native scroll already targets the top, so both agree. `animated: false` matches the rows and needs no reduced-motion branch. The native Up scroll stays the platform's smooth scroll; turning it off (`scrollAnimationEnabled`) would change every vertical list and is not needed.
- **Not chosen for the fade:**
  - Hide the fade when scrolled to the end. It removes the 96 px left margin while there (cause 4c).
  - Draw a gradient overlay. A native fade shows the backdrop through; a painted gradient cannot match a backdrop that changes.
  - `snapToAlignment: "item"` with `snapToItemPadding`. It changes every row's scrolling and smooth-scrolls.
- **Milestones:**
  1. Hero guide and `revealHeader`.
  2. Row end padding and fade.
  3. Emulator evidence (below). `pnpm check` green after 1 and 2.

**Verdict: Changes needed.**

1. **Medium. Up may not end at Home's top: the vertical list's own smooth scroll can undo `revealHeader`.** Up between rows is handled by the vertical ScrollView before any view-tree search: `ScrollView.dispatchKeyEvent` → `executeKeyEvent` → `arrowScroll(FOCUS_UP)` (AOSP; `ReactScrollView.kt:513-538`, `:750-758`). `arrowScroll` finds the next focus inside the list, then calls `doScrollY` to show *that* view's rect. With smooth scrolling on (the ScrollView default; RN's `scrollAnimationEnabled` also defaults to true, `ReactScrollView.kt:215`, so `computeScroll` does not jump, `:575-587`), that is a ~250 ms `OverScroller` animation. Only then does it call `requestFocus` on the next view. The JS `scrollToOffset(0, animated: false)` reaches `scrollTo` (`:1241-1245`), which does not stop the `OverScroller`. A `revealHeader` that lands inside that window is pulled back to the bar-only offset on the next `computeScroll`. `media-row.tsx`'s scroll home does not prove the pattern: there the native target and the JS target are both 0. Cause 3's last bullet also names only `requestChildFocus`; the first scroll is `arrowScroll`'s. **Fix, either:** (a) make the guide the whole hero (title to action bar, `trapFocusRight`, destination the opening button; the slots keep their own `actions` parent, so `centreOf` and T5-03 item 10 hold). Then `arrowScroll` scrolls to the hero's rect, which is its top at offset 0, and native and JS agree. The beam and Right arguments are unchanged, because the hero spans the same width as the bar.
