## Goal

Make Continue watching the row the owner decided (`docs/specs/decisions-owner-inbox-2026-11-22.md` §3; `docs/specs/decisions-owner-chat-2026-11-22.md` §1; `PRODUCT.md:36`; `DESIGN.md:183-184`):

1. A title leaves the row once it was last played longer ago than the account's window. The device works this out from `UserData.LastPlayedDate` and the Clock port. Nothing on the server is hidden, reset or written.
2. The window is 30, 90, 180 or 365 days, or Never, and 90 days when the account has none set.
3. The row shows at most 20 cards, most recently played first, after ageing and merging. This replaces T5-02's 16.

Most of the machinery exists: `continueWatchingRow`, `windowStart` and `ContinueWindow` in `packages/core/src/domain/up-next.ts`, and `composeHome` and `loadHomeLists` in `packages/core/src/domain/home.ts` (T5-02). What is missing: the owner's window values and default, the cap of 20, Home's requests asking for 20, a single entry point that reads `now` from the Clock port, and tests of all of it against the three servers' recordings.

## Decisions

Technical choices made here; the Product Owner may overrule any of them, the owner may veto any of them.

- **D1. The window values.** `ContinueWindow = "30" | "90" | "180" | "365" | "all"`, exported as `CONTINUE_WINDOWS` in that order from `up-next.ts`. `"all"` is the owner's **Never**: it stays spelled `"all"` because T5-02's frozen tests pass it, and the label "Never" is T9-03's. `DEFAULT_CONTINUE_WINDOW = "90"`. `windowStart` counts back `days × 86 400 000 ms` from `now` and has no start for `"all"`.
- **D2. "None set" and anything unknown read as 90.** New `continueWindowOrDefault(value: unknown): ContinueWindow` in `up-next.ts` returns the value when it is one of `CONTINUE_WINDOWS`, else `"90"`. `composeHome` takes `window?: ContinueWindow` and passes it through `continueWindowOrDefault`, so `undefined` (the account has none), a stale `"7"`/`"14"` and garbage all give 90 days. `preference-store.ts`'s `sanitizeContinueWindow` delegates to it, so the store's list and default cannot drift from the domain's. This replaces T5-02 D1 ("no default"): acceptance item 2 now names the default.
- **D3. Which date a card ages by** (acceptance item 3):
  - **A Resume card** (a film or an episode in progress) ages by its own `UserData.LastPlayedDate`.
  - **A next-episode card** ages by its series' last play: the newest `LastPlayedDate` among the series' finished episodes in the recently-played list. It also stays while the episode itself is new: when its `DateCreated` is inside the window and later than that last play, the viewer had caught up and a new episode arrived, so the card stays until a window after the episode arrived. This is the rule `up-next.ts` already builds (owner answer Q14 B of 2026-11-15 for the desktop app, commit `0c4a8bf7`, `docs/specs/P16-02.md` D5; `docs/research/discovery-api.md` §3). The 2026-11-22 answer ("drops a title 90 days after it was last played") does not address a new episode, and a new episode has not been played, so this spec keeps the existing rule. Kept cards sort by the series' last play.
  - **A card with no date:**
    - a Resume card with no `LastPlayedDate` is kept and sorts after every dated card. Hiding needs a date; a missing date never hides a title.
    - a next-episode card whose series' last play cannot be known (the recently-played list failed, or it is full and its oldest entry is inside the window) is kept, undated, after the dated cards and after undated Resume cards.
    - a next-episode card whose series is absent from a **complete** recently-played list, or from a full list whose oldest entry is before the window, has no play inside the window: it is kept only by the new-episode clause, else aged out.
  - The boundary: a date exactly at the window's start is kept; one millisecond older is aged out.
- **D4. The cap counts the hero, as T5-02 D3 did.** `CONTINUE_ROW_LIMIT = 20`. The merged, aged list is cut at 20, and then the hero (when it is the row's first title) is taken out, so the row beneath it shows at most 19 cards and the row plus the hero hold at most 20 titles. Order is unchanged: dated cards newest first, then undated Resume cards, then undated next episodes, ties in arrival order.
- **D5. Home asks for 20 of each list.** `resumeItems(signal?, limit = HOME_ROW_LIMIT)` and `nextUp(signal?, limit = HOME_ROW_LIMIT)` gain an optional `limit`. `HomeSource` declares the same optional parameter, and `loadHomeLists` passes `CONTINUE_ROW_LIMIT` for `resume` and `nextUp`. Why: with 16 of each, a viewer with only films in progress could never see 20. A call with no limit keeps 16, so T2-04's frozen golden and `latestInLibrary` do not change. Resume is answered newest last play first (`discovery-api.md` §2), so the titles the window ages out are the tail the limit would drop anyway. `recentlyPlayed` stays at 100.
- **D6. The Clock port reaches the row through one entry point.** New in `home.ts`: `export interface HomeClock { now(): number }` (the core's `Clock` satisfies it structurally, as `JellyfinGateway` satisfies `HomeSource`; the domain imports no port) and
  ```ts
  export async function loadHome(
    source: HomeSource,
    options: { window?: ContinueWindow; clock: HomeClock },
    signal?: AbortSignal,
  ): Promise<{ lists: HomeLists; failed: HomeListFailure[]; home: Home }>;
  ```
  It runs `loadHomeLists(source, HOME_LISTS, signal)`, reads `clock.now()` once after the lists settle, and returns `composeHome(lists, { window, now })`. `loadHomeLists` and `composeHome` stay, for T5-04's one-list retry. T5-03 calls `loadHome` with the host clock; T9-09/T9-03 supply the account's window.
- **D7. Only hidden.** Ageing is a pure filter on each load. No state is kept between loads, so a title whose `LastPlayedDate` moves inside the window (played again, on any device) is back on the next load. Home sends only `GET` requests; no `nextUpDateCutoff`, no `UserData` write, no hide call.
- **D8. `DateCreated` stays on Next Up.** It is read by D3's new-episode clause. This settles T5-02's backlog line on it (keep). The Next Up `fields` value does not change (frozen in T5-02's `home-lists.test.ts`).
