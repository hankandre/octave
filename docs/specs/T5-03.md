## Goal

Draw Home from what T5-02 and T5-07 compose in the core, so that "Resume takes one press" (`PRODUCT.md` promise 1, `DESIGN.md` §2 principle 2 and §12 decision 1) holds on the screen:

1. Home opens with focus on the hero's Resume (or Play). One select hands the hero's title and its saved position to the playback use case.
2. Below the hero, always in this order: Continue watching (thumb cards that play in one press), then Recently added (poster cards that open the title's page). An empty row is hidden; the other keeps its place.
3. The ambient backdrop follows the focused card after a 300 ms dwell and crossfades, or swaps at once under reduced motion.
4. Nothing reorders once shown, nothing plays by itself, and Home builds no media URL.
5. The hero's overview and quality facts come from one item read of the hero (D4), and focus entering the rows from the hero lands on the card nearest the focused hero button, never on a destination left from an earlier visit (D8).

## Decisions

Technical and placement choices made here. The Product Owner may overrule any of them; the owner may veto any of them.

- **D1. Home is built and tested, not yet wired into `App.tsx`.** There is no session to read Home with until sign-in (T4) or T8-03's slice wiring. `App.tsx` keeps `FirstScreen` on every route, so `pnpm tv:smoke`'s `tile-0` oracle keeps passing. Home takes everything it needs from a `HomeServicesContext` (D2), so the composition root wires it in one place later; the implementer appends the backlog line for it (below). The artwork provider is wired with it, as T3-11 expected (`docs/specs/T3-11.md:79`).
- **D2. Home's inputs are one context value.** `DOMAINS.md` §8 and dependency rule 4: screens get use cases through props or context and construct no gateway.

  ```ts
  // apps/tv/src/home/home-services.tsx
  export type HomeServices = {
    source: HomeSource & HeroSource; // the signed-in gateway satisfies both structurally
    clock: HomeClock;                // hostClock in the app
    window?: () => ContinueWindow | undefined; // the account's window, read once per load; undefined gives 90 days (T5-07 D2)
    play(request: PlayRequest): void; // the playback use case (T8-03); a fake in this task's tests
  };
  export const HomeServicesContext: React.Context<HomeServices | undefined>;
  ```

  With no services Home renders an empty `surface0` screen and requests nothing.
- **D3. The play hand-off.** New in the core, `packages/core/src/domain/home-hero.ts`:

  ```ts
  export interface PlayRequest { itemId: string; start: PlaybackStartChoice }
  export function playRequestOf(card: MediaCard): PlayRequest | undefined;
  ```

  A card whose `resumePositionTicks` is above zero gives `{ kind: "resume", startPositionTicks }`; any other gives `startOverChoice()` (0). A `Series` or `BoxSet` card gives `undefined` (nothing to play directly). A select on the hero's primary or on a Continue watching card calls `services.play(request)` once, synchronously, and does nothing else: no route, no dialog, no player call. Opening the player is the use case's job (T8-03). A Continue watching card of kind `Other` (a home video in the Resume list) plays like a film (backlog line 280 stays with the Product Owner for the hero rule).
- **D4. Where the hero's overview and quality facts come from (acceptance item 9).** After the lists settle, Home reads **one item**: `itemDetail(hero.card.id)` through `HeroSource`. It is the existing request (`GET /Items/{id}?userId=`), already recorded on Jellyfin 10.11.7, 12.4.0 and the pinned Jellyscale, and T8-03's play path may read the same item first; no cache shares the answer between the two, so that saving is **unverified**. Its full body is heavier than the two facts need; `/Items/{id}` takes no `fields` (`gateway.ts:705-707`). A narrower read is a backlog line, not this task. For a **Series** hero (a `"newest"` pick on Jellyfin, where Recently added holds grouped shows) Home also reads `seriesNextUp(seriesId)` in parallel, because a series cannot be handed to playback (D3). The hero's text:
  - **Title**: the card's `title` in `typeHero`, at most two lines (D9 covers the Logo).
  - **Facts line** (`DESIGN.md` §7.3), parts joined with `" · "`, built by `heroFacts(card, detail?, next?)` in `home-hero.ts`:
    - Episode: `S{season} · E{episode}` when both numbers are known, then the time left (`"38 min left"`) when the card has `remainingTicks`, else the runtime from the detail (`"45 min"`).
    - Movie: the year (`detail.year`, else the card's year subtitle), the runtime (`runtimeLabel(detail.runTimeTicks)`), then the quality facts of the first version: `"4K"` when its video is at least 3200 px wide or 1800 px tall, and the range: `"Dolby Vision"` for any `Dolby Vision…` value, else `"HDR10+"`, `"HDR10"`, `"HLG"` or `"HDR"` as mapped; nothing for `SDR` or `Unknown`. These are the words of `DESIGN.md` §7.9's badges. This follows §7.3's example literally (`2023 · 2 h 3 min · 4K · Dolby Vision`), so an in-progress film shows its runtime, not its time left.
    - Series: the year, or `{year}–{endYear}` when the series ended, then the next episode's `S{s} · E{e}` when known.
    - Before the detail arrives, the facts are what the card alone gives (the episode numbers and time left, or a film's year).
  - **Overview**: `detail.overview`, at most two lines in `typeBodyLg` and `text2`, as wide as the `measure` token allows.
  - A failed or 404 detail leaves the card's facts and no overview; nothing is shown about the failure (T5-04 owns error copy). An aborted read is dropped.
- **D5. The hero's actions** (`DESIGN.md` §7.3, §7.19). The primary button reads **"Resume"** when the request's start is a resume, else **"Play"**, and takes `hasTVPreferredFocus`; it is reported as the screen's `opening` view. Beside it, **"More info"** (secondary) opens `{ kind: "item", id: hero.card.id }`. A Series hero's primary hands its next episode (`playRequestOf` of the `SeriesNextUp`, label by its position). When the series read gives no episode (failed, empty, or Jellyscale's stub), the hero shows only "More info", as the primary variant with the preferred focus: there is nothing to play, and `DESIGN.md` §6 says a screen without a primary action focuses its first control.
- **D6. The rows.** Two `RowSpec`s, always in this order, with stable ids: `continue-watching` ("Continue watching", `thumb`) and `recently-added` ("Recently added", `poster`) (`DESIGN.md` §7.4 titles). `RowList` hides an empty one. Cards map in `apps/tv/src/home/home-cards.ts`:
  - Continue watching (`thumb`, `DESIGN.md` §7.5): art `thumb ?? backdrop ?? parentBackdrop ?? image`; title the card's title (the series name for an episode); subtitle `S1 · E2 · 38 min left` (numbers when known, time left when `remainingTicks`), else the card's own subtitle; `progress`, `played`; accessibility label "{title}, {the card's subtitle with each ` · ` read as a space}, {38 minutes left}" (§7.5's example: "Harbor Lights, S1 E2 The Keeper's Log, 38 minutes left"). Select: D3.
  - Recently added (`poster`): art `image`; title; subtitle the year when the card has one; `played`. Select opens `{ kind: "item", id: card.id }`: the film's page for a Movie and the series page for a Series (T5-02 already gives a grouped episode its series id).
  - Time left: whole minutes rounded, at least 1: `"{m} min left"` under an hour, `"{h} h {m} min left"` above (`"{h} h left"` at a whole hour); spoken `"{m} minutes left"`, `"1 minute left"`, `"{h} hours {m} minutes left"`. `timeLeftLabel` and `timeLeftSpoken` live in `home-hero.ts`.
- **D7. Rows wait for their data (acceptance item 7).** Home has one state reducer (a `switch`, `AGENTS.md` code style): `loading` until `loadHome` resolves (and, for a Series hero, its next-episode read settles), then `ready` with the composed `Home`, set once. Nothing is drawn before `ready` (T5-04 adds the skeleton after 150 ms and the error rows). The rows' item arrays are never replaced while Home is mounted: the hero detail arriving later changes only the hero's facts and overview, in slots whose heights are fixed at one facts line and two overview lines, so neither the hero's buttons nor the rows move when it arrives. A fresh mount loads again; a Back to Home keeps the mounted screen (refreshing after playback is T8-03 item 7).
- **D8. Where focus lands when it enters the rows from the hero (acceptance item 10).** `DESIGN.md` §9: "Up and Down across rows go to the nearest item by horizontal centre", and §7.4: "to the card nearest by horizontal centre". So Down from a hero button lands on the card of the **first shown row** (Continue watching, or Recently added when Continue watching is empty) whose centre is nearest the focused button's centre, at that row's current scroll offset. On a fresh visit (offset 0) that is the row's first card. Built as:
  - `RowRouter.enter(rowId, x)` (new, `row-router.ts`): `x` in canvas px; it points that row's guide at the card `nearestCardIndex(variant, count, memory.get(rowId) / canvasScale(), x)` names (an empty list when that card has no view) and forgets the last focused card, so a later `scrolled()` does not re-point from it.
  - `RowList` gains an optional `entryRef?: React.RefObject<RowListEntry | null>` with `RowListEntry = { fromHeader(x: number): void }`, which calls `router.clear()` and then `router.enter(firstShownRow.id, x)`.
  - Each hero button reports its focus: `useFocusPlace().focused("hero-<action>", view)`, then `entryRef.current?.fromHeader(centreX)`, where `centreX` is the button's horizontal centre in canvas px.
  - `Button` and `FocusFrame` take no `onLayout`, so the hero wraps each button in a plain `View` that takes it: `home-hero-primary-slot` and `home-hero-more-info-slot`. The `layout` event of that wrapper gives `x` and `width` in dp, **relative to the action bar** (the row of buttons), not to the canvas. The action bar sits in the hero's column, which starts `rowInsetStart` in from the left (the rows' own x frame, `row-geometry.ts`) and has no padding of its own. So the centre is `rowInsetStart` plus the slot's `x` plus half its `width`, all divided by `canvasScale()`: `(rowInsetStart + x + width / 2) / canvasScale()`, with `rowInsetStart` taken in dp (`px(rowInsetStart)`) as the action bar's offset. The test builds the same sum from the `layout` event it fires. Before a slot has reported a layout, the centre is `rowInsetStart` (the first button's left edge), so a focus that beats the layout still lands on card 0.
  - `Button` gains two optional props passed to `FocusFrame`: `onFocus` and `viewRef`. Existing callers are unchanged.
- **D9. The Logo is not drawn yet; the title is text.** `DESIGN.md` §4 and §12 decision 3 make the Logo the hero's title. The artwork slot draws a `surface2` box until the image loads and reports only `failed` (T3-11), and its props are frozen (T3-08 fixtures), so a Logo would show as a grey box over the backdrop first, and on Jellyscale (which answers 404 for `Logo`, `docs/specs/T3-11.md:233`) always fall back. The owner chose this on 2026-11-24 (open question 1, answered).
- **D10. The ambient backdrop** (`DESIGN.md` §7.2, acceptance item 6). `apps/tv/src/home/ambient-backdrop.tsx`, absolutely behind Home's content:
  - The art is the target's `backdrop ?? parentBackdrop`, drawn through the artwork slot at the canvas size (1920 × 1080 canvas px; the native cap is 1920, `docs/specs/T3-11.md:133`), at 35% opacity (a named constant citing §7.2), under `scrimHero` and `scrimFloor` drawn from the depth tokens with the `backgroundImage` linear-gradient style. The art tint (`artTint`, `artGlow`, §3.2) is not built; the scrims use the token colours only (backlog).
  - The target is the hero when Home becomes `ready` (shown at once, no dwell) and when a hero button takes focus; a card's own art when a card takes focus. A target with no backdrop shows no art: the layer never keeps another title's picture.
  - `useDwell(target, motion.dwellMs)` hands a new target on only after it has held for 300 ms; each focus change restarts the wait.
  - A settled change crossfades over `motion.durSlow` with `motion.easeInOut` (`Animated.timing`, native driver), the new layer over the old, and the old layer is dropped when the fade ends. Under reduced motion the old layer is replaced in the same render and no animation runs (the acceptance item's "swaps without a fade"; stricter than §5's "120 ms or less, or instant").
- **D11. New code goes in new files** under `apps/tv/src/home/` (one screen folder, as `first-screen/` is), each under about 400 lines, and `packages/core/src/domain/home-hero.ts`. `home.ts`, `up-next.ts` and `gateway.ts` do not change.
