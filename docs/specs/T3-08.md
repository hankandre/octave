## Goal

Every browse screen (Home in T5-03, search, the series page, the library) builds its rows from one `MediaRow` and one `MediaCard`. Together they carry decision 0004's measured focus fixes, so focus moves exactly where the viewer pressed:

1. A row moves one card per Left or Right press, and a held key never loses or doubles a move.
2. A row's ends hold focus. With no rail, Left on the first card stays put. The Left edge opens for T3-09's rail.
3. Up and Down land on the adjacent row's card nearest the same horizontal position.
4. Each row keeps its own scroll position in screen state, keyed by a stable row id. A recycled row never inherits another row's place (decision 0004, condition 6).
5. The focused card is never recycled under the viewer.
6. Poster and thumb cards show the title, the progress bar and the played mark. A missing image shows the title on a plain surface. Artwork comes through a slot that T3-11's native image view fills, so no URL ever reaches a view.

## Facts this spec rests on

Each was read on 2026-11-22. "Unverified" marks what could not be checked here; the step named proves it.

- **The spike's six focus defects and their fixes** (`RESULTS.md`, "Defects found", "Costs to carry into decision 0004"; `src/App.tsx`, arm `traps`, `rowMemory`, `mvcpOff`):
  1. A focused cell was recycled and the row snapped back, giving a BLUR with no FOCUS. It went (0 recycled-cell BLURs) with `maintainVisibleContentPosition={{ disabled: true }}` on the rows.
  2. Left on the rail escaped into the content. That is T3-09's to fix.
  3. Left at column 0 sometimes did not reach the rail, because the row still had left padding to scroll. Fix: when a row's first card takes focus, `scrollToOffset({ offset: 0, animated: false })`.
  4. Right past a row's end left the row. Fix: `TVFocusGuideView trapFocusRight` around each row's list.
  5. A row's scroll offset leaked into other rows. Fix: per-row offsets restored in `useLayoutEffect` when the vertical list hands a row view to another row. The spike restored only on that hand-over, not on a fresh mount.
  6. There was no initial focus after `am start`. That is T3-09's (opening focus).

- **Up and Down were never checked for "nearest".** The spike oracle accepted "any card in the adjacent row" (`RESULTS.md`, Focus test). In the three F1 emulator runs, `column_offset_on_row_change` was `{n: 95, same_col: 86, max_abs: 43}`, `{97, 51, 40}` and `{79, 70, 29}` (`results/emu-F1*-paced-*/focus.json`). Large offsets are expected when rows are scrolled to different places, but the data neither proves nor refutes the nearest-centre rule. Android's `FocusFinder` prefers candidates in the source's beam and then the smallest `13·major² + minor²`, where the minor axis is the distance between centres. For rows of equal height that is the nearest card by horizontal centre (AOSP `FocusFinder.java`, recalled, **unverified** here). Whether react-native-tvos on Fabric keeps that, including next to the lift transform, is **unverified**. Milestone 5 checks it on the emulator ("Approach", "Up and Down").
- **How a focused card scrolls into view.** `ReactHorizontalScrollView.requestChildFocus` calls `scrollToChild`, which scrolls by `computeScrollDeltaToGetChildRectOnScreen` (react-native-tvos 0.88.1, `ReactAndroid/.../scroll/ReactHorizontalScrollView.kt:350-355, 603-611, 682-687`). Android's base method keeps the child inside the view, less the horizontal fading edge length on each side that still has content to scroll. That length is non-zero only when `fadingEdgeLength` is set. The manager sets it as one length, `max(start, end)` (`ReactHorizontalScrollViewManager.kt:378-409`; `ScrollView.js:473` takes a number or `{start, end}`). React Native draws the fade per side (`ReactHorizontalScrollView.kt:425-436`), but the margin applies to both sides (AOSP `HorizontalScrollView`, recalled, **unverified**). So:
  - by default a card moving in from the right stops flush with the screen's right edge, and DESIGN.md §9's "at least 96 px from any edge" is not met there. It is met on the left, because the row's viewport starts at the safe inset;
  - with `fadingEdgeLength={{ start: 0, end: 96 px }}`, a card is kept 96 px inside the row on both sides, but only the right side draws a fade. On the left the focused card then stops 96 px inside the row's viewport, which is 192 px from the screen edge, except for the first card at offset 0.

| `RowMemory`, `createRowMemory`, `useRowMemory` (`row-memory.ts`) | `RowMemory = { get(rowId: string): number; set(rowId: string, offset: number): void }`. The offset is the list's scroll offset in dp, as `onScroll` reports it and `scrollToOffset` takes it. `get` returns 0 for an unknown id. `useRowMemory()` returns one memory per mounted screen |
| `row-geometry.ts` | `ROW_DRAW_DISTANCE = 150` (dp). In canvas px: `cardWidth(variant)`, `cardGap`, `rowInsetStart`, `cardCentre(variant, index, offset)` and `nearestCardIndex(variant, count, offset, x): number`, where `offset` is the row's scroll offset in canvas px |

**Units.** The memory and the lists work in dp; the geometry works in canvas px. Anything that joins them, which means the Up and Down fallback, the milestone 5 check and T3-10's oracle, converts a remembered offset with `offset / canvasScale()` (`scale.ts`) before calling `nearestCardIndex`. `row-geometry.ts` imports only `../tokens`, so a Node script can import it. The caller supplies the scale: 0.5 on the 960 dp window of a 1080p TV.

testIDs, with `r` a row's `testID`, `l` a `RowList`'s, and `c` a card's:

- `r` is the row container; `r-title` its title; `r-guide` the `TVFocusGuideView`; `r-list` the horizontal FlashList; `r-card-<index>` each card, passed as the card's `testID`.
- `l-list` is the vertical FlashList, and `l-row-<rowId>` each row's `testID`.
- `c` is the card's `Pressable` (the one focusable element, with `focusable` set to `true` explicitly, since `focusableElements()` reads that prop); `c-art` the art frame; `c-focus-ring` the ring, only while focused; `c-artwork` the slot's component; `c-missing` the title-on-surface; `c-title`; `c-subtitle`; `c-progress` the track; `c-progress-fill`; `c-played`.

## Approach

### Card (`MediaCard`)

- **One focusable element.** A `Pressable` with `focusable` true, `accessibilityRole="button"` and `accessibilityLabel` set to `item.accessibilityLabel` or, failing that, the title and subtitle joined with ", " (DESIGN.md §7.5). The screen tasks add the progress words ("38 minutes left"); this task adds no copy. `onPress` is select. Holding select for details belongs to the screen tasks.
- **Focus look: F4, static** (decision 0004 addendum; `docs/specs/T3-06.md`, "The cards (T3-08) follow the addendum"). Focus state comes from the `Pressable`'s `onFocus` and `onBlur`. While focused, the art frame gets:
  - `transform: [{ scale: motion.lift }]`, set at once, with no `Animated` and no Reanimated;
  - `FocusRing` with `radius="radiusArt"`;
  - the shadow: `shadowLift` as a `boxShadow` (owner answer 4), built as `dialog.tsx` builds `shadowSheet`.

  The title goes from `text2` to `text1`. There is no press scale: F4 measured none. Under reduced motion there is no scale; the ring and shadow still show (DESIGN.md §5).
- **Frame.** `poster`: `px(sizes.posterW)` by `px(sizes.posterW * 1.5)`. `thumb`: `px(sizes.thumbW)` by `px(sizes.thumbW * 9 / 16)`. Radius `radiusArt`, `overflow: "hidden"` for the art, with the ring outside the clip. The frame is a child of the `Pressable`, so the ring and lift sit on the art only, not on the text under it.
- **Under the art.** `c-title` in `typeLabel`, `numberOfLines={1}` (DESIGN.md §4). `c-subtitle` in `typeMeta`, `text2`, one line, only when given. The space between art and title must clear the lift: half of 8 % of the poster's height is about 15 px, plus the ring's 7 px. So it is `space5` (owner answer 1).
- **Marks.**
  - `c-played` is a `markDisc` circle (`radiusPill`) on `veil`, `space3` in from the top-right corner. Inside it, the card renders `useArtwork().playedIcon` at `px(sizes.markDisc)` less `space1` on each side, in `text1`. With no icon given, the disc shows empty (owner answer 3; the check waits for the Tabler set).
  - `c-progress` is a `cardProgressH` track along the frame's bottom edge in `colors.track`, with `c-progress-fill` at `${progress * 100}%` in `artDefaults.artAccent`.
  - The unplayed-episode count is deferred ("Out of scope").
- **Artwork.** `useArtwork()` reads the provider. With a `component` and an `item.image`, the card renders `<Artwork image={item.image} width={canvasW} height={canvasH} onFailed={...} testID={`${c}-artwork`} />` absolutely filling the frame. The fill goes on a wrapping `View`, never as a `style` prop on the slot component, because item 6 freezes exactly its five props.
  - The slot component sits in a wrapping `View` with the absolute fill: item 6 freezes exactly `image`, `width`, `height`, `onFailed` and `testID` on it, so no `style` prop goes on it.
  - `onFailed` sets local state that switches the frame to `c-missing`. The state resets when `item.id` changes, so a recycled card does not inherit a failure.
  - `c-missing` is `surface2` with the title centred in `typeLabel`, `text2`, up to two lines.
  - Sizes are canvas px, the drawn size, so T3-11 requests "at the size they are drawn".
  - The slot carries no URL, header, token or session: T3-11's provider binds the session natively.
- `MediaCard` is `memo`'d on `item`, `variant`, `testID` and the callbacks. FlashList recycles it, so it holds no per-item state other than the failure flag, which is keyed by `item.id`.

### Row (`MediaRow`)

- A title (`r-title`, `typeHeading`, `text1`, not focusable) with 16 px (`space4`) between it and the cards (owner answer 1), then `TVFocusGuideView` `r-guide` with `trapFocusRight` and, when `leftEdge` is `"trap"`, `trapFocusLeft`. Inside it is the horizontal `FlashList` `r-list`:
  - `horizontal`, `data={items}`, `keyExtractor={(item) => item.id}`;
  - `maintainVisibleContentPosition={{ disabled: true }}` and `drawDistance={ROW_DRAW_DISTANCE}`;
  - `showsHorizontalScrollIndicator={false}`;
  - `contentContainerStyle` with the leading inset `px(rowInsetStart)`. Each card cell is `px(cardWidth + cardGap)` wide;
  - a list height that holds the lifted card, the ring and the text: `frameH * motion.lift + 2 * (focusOffset + focusRingWidth + focusEdgeWidth)` plus the text block;
  - `renderItem` passes `testID={`${r}-card-${index}`}` and callbacks bound to `(rowId, index)`.
- **Focus reporting.** A card's `onFocus` calls `onCardFocus(rowId, index)` with the row id and index it renders now. If `index === 0`, it then calls `listRef.current?.scrollToOffset({ offset: 0, animated: false })` and `memory.set(rowId, 0)` (spike defect 3). `onBlur` calls `onCardBlur(rowId, index)` in the same way.
- **Row memory.** `shownId = useRef(rowId)`. `onScroll` sets `memory.set(shownId.current, x)`. `useLayoutEffect` on `[rowId]`: when `rowId !== shownId.current`, set `shownId.current = rowId` and call `scrollToOffset({ offset: memory.get(rowId), animated: false })`. On mount, when `memory.get(rowId) > 0`, it restores the same way. The hand-over restore is the spike's measured `rowMemory` arm, keyed by a stable id where the spike used a module global keyed by index. The fresh-mount restore is new and is unmeasured on a device (milestone 5).
- **The right edge.** The row has the right-edge treatment the owner chose (answer 2, option A): `fadingEdgeLength={{ start: 0, end: px(space.safeX) }}`. The prop takes dp (`ReactHorizontalScrollViewManager.kt:406` converts with `.dpToPx()`), and `px()` already returns dp, so no further conversion is applied. FlashList 2.3.3 passes it through: `RecyclerView.js:357` spreads `...rest` onto its `ScrollView` (verified in the spec check), so no `renderScrollComponent` fallback is needed.
- An empty `items` renders `null`, so the row is hidden (DESIGN.md §7.4).

### Rows (`RowList`)

- A vertical `FlashList` `l-list`: `data={rows.filter((row) => row.items.length > 0)}`, `keyExtractor={(row) => row.id}`, `getItemType={(row) => row.variant}`, and `ListHeaderComponent={header}` for T5-03's hero. Each row is `<MediaRow testID={`${l}-row-${row.id}`} ... />` with a bottom margin of `px(sizes.rowGap)`. The vertical list keeps FlashList's default draw distance and maintain-visible-position setting, as every measured build did.
- `leftEdge`, the callbacks and `memory` pass through to every row.
- The screen owns the memory: `const memory = useRowMemory()`. A fresh visit gets a fresh memory (DESIGN.md §9: "A fresh visit never restores"). Keeping it across Back is T3-09's.

### Up and Down

- **Primary path: the native engine.** Nothing overrides vertical focus. The rule it should follow is `nearestCardIndex`: in the target row, the card whose centre x, after that row's offset, is nearest the source card's centre x, with a tie going to the lower index. The lift transform is not part of the geometry.
- **Milestone 5 checks it.** On the scratch fixture screen, with rows scrolled to different offsets and a thumb row between poster rows, at least 20 Up and Down presses each land on `nearestCardIndex`'s card.
- **Fallback, only if milestone 5 shows a miss.** Each row's `r-guide` gets `destinations` set to the nearest card of that row, computed with `nearestCardIndex` (offsets converted as in "Units") whenever a card in an adjacent row takes focus. The card views are held in a map keyed by row id and item id, and an entry is dropped when its view shows another item. The implementer records the miss and the switch in the decision log. The fallback has a known risk: under a fast held key, JavaScript can set a destination late. Milestone 5 then repeats its 12 held Down bursts. The frozen tests hold on either path.

### Geometry (`row-geometry.ts`)

All of it is canvas px, except `ROW_DRAW_DISTANCE`, which is dp. The row converts with `px()` when it lays out.

- `cardGap` is `space.space5` (owner answer 1).
- `rowInsetStart` is the row's leading inset inside the content area. It must be large enough that the first card's lift and ring are not clipped: `focusOffset + focusRingWidth + focusEdgeWidth + (posterW * (lift - 1)) / 2`, rounded up to the 4 px grid.
- `cardCentre(variant, index, offset) = rowInsetStart + index * (cardWidth(variant) + cardGap) + cardWidth(variant) / 2 - offset`.
- `nearestCardIndex` clamps to `[0, count - 1]`.

### Decision log

1. The rows use FlashList with decision 0004's F1 fixes and F4 settings: row MVCP off, `drawDistance` 150 dp, `trapFocusRight` and the first-card scroll home, per-row offsets, and a static lift. Each one is measured on poster rows; the rest of the primary path adds only the fresh-mount restore.
2. The native engine moves focus. A JavaScript-driven spatial navigation library (one that intercepts keys and moves focus itself) was not chosen: decision 0004 measured the native engine clean with these fixes, and a switch would be an unmeasured architecture change.
3. Row memory is screen state keyed by row id (`useRowMemory`), in the list's dp, not a module global.
4. Artwork is a slot whose props are an `ImageRef` and a drawn size; T3-11 fills it. The same provider carries the played glyph, so a screen sets up one provider.
5. `FocusRing` is extracted from `FocusFrame`, so the card reuses the ring without the animated lift.
6. Two tokens record DESIGN.md §7.5's existing values: `markDisc` 24 and `cardProgressH` 4.
7. The look choices are the owner's four answers of 2026-11-22 ("Owner decisions (look)"), recorded in DESIGN.md §12. A later different answer changes non-frozen code and DESIGN.md only.
8. The implementer appends: whether FlashList passes `fadingEdgeLength` through, the milestone 5 results (thumb-row bursts and the fresh-mount restore included), and whether the Up and Down fallback was needed.

9. `rowInsetStart` uses the wider of the poster and the thumb (`thumbW`), not `posterW` as "Geometry" says, so a thumb row's first lifted card is not clipped; rows share one x frame. The cards also get `cardTopInset` of headroom above the art (the row title's bottom margin is reduced by it, so the art sits `space4` under the title).
10. `fadingEdgeLength` reaches the real `ScrollView` through FlashList (`RecyclerView.js:357`); no `renderScrollComponent` fallback.
11. **Milestone 5, first run (emulator, 1920x1080 at 320 dpi, scratch screen of 5 rows of 50, row 2 thumb, release APK, `monkey -f` bursts at 50 ms).** A poster row at `drawDistance` 150 did NOT hold: 12 bursts of 49 Right ended on card 49 in 5 of 12 (the others on 41 to 48). Raw log shows two defects: the first Right of a burst sometimes focused card 7 then 1 then 7 before stepping, and after reaching card 49 focus later jumped to card 42, about 0.7 s after the last press, with no blur logged without a focus (0 blurs without focus in 646 focus events). Under the Product Owner ruling, a poster row losing a press at 150 means stop and ask; nothing was changed to hide it. The thumb-row bursts, the Up and Down oracle and the fresh-mount check were not run. Differences from the spike's F4 build that are not yet ruled out: `fadingEdgeLength`, the per-card cell `View`, the focus-state `setState` in each card, `TVFocusGuideView` `trapFocusLeft`, and the missing `maxItemsInRecyclePool`. ## Risks and out of scope

**Risks**

- **Up and Down may not be "nearest" natively** (Facts). The emulator checks it, and the fallback is stated, along with its own held-key risk.

Milestone 5 runs the held bursts on a thumb row too. Product Owner ruling (2026-11-22): item 1 freezes the exact 150 on a poster row only; for a thumb row it freezes a number of at least `ROW_DRAW_DISTANCE`. Every variant stays at 150 unless milestone 5 measures a lost press in the thumb-row bursts: then the smallest value that measures 0 of 12, at most 243 dp (150 dp to a 136 dp poster stride, applied to the 220 dp thumb stride), as a named constant in `row-geometry.ts`. Both runs go in the decision log and the PR, with a backlog line asking T3-12 and T3-18 to measure its memory cost. If 243 dp still loses a press, or a poster row loses one at 150, stop and ask. - **The F4 draw distance figure is the owner's report** of a run whose file is not in the repository (decision 0004 addendum, **unverified**). Milestone 5's bursts are this task's own check, and T3-10 is the lasting one.
