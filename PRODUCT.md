# Product

A starter. It states only what the owner has already decided; the rest comes through proposals the owner approves.

## What Octave is

Octave is the client for jellyscale: a private collection of films and shows, shared with a household, friends and family, or a community.

- **Video only.** Films and shows; no music or photos.
- **Top-tier, not passable.** The goal is a top-tier client, not a passable one.
- **Native apps on each platform.** Android TV first to reach viewers, alongside Android phones, the web, and iPhone and iPad. Apple TV comes later.
- **One design language.** Every client shares one consistent design language.

## Two products

Octave and jellyscale are separate products in separate repositories, each with its own owner, plan and releases. jellyscale's published API contract is the source of truth for the server.
